Wear OS · concept draft · v5

Live Vienna departures, on the wrist

A watch app for the real-time Wiener Linien data Google Maps skips. Open the app, hit your location, find the nearest stop, pick your line, read the countdown. Swipe flips direction; the crown walks to the next stop. Pin the lines you ride to a honeycomb tile.

Wiener Linien OGD · no API key Kotlin · Compose for Wear OS app finds & manages · tile is the saved set

01 The app

The app opens on Nearby. Swipe left or right to reach favorites and search — leaving up/down free to scroll those lists. Tap into any stop, pick a line, and read the times. On the times screen: swipe flips direction (H ↔ R), the crown steps to farther stops. Scroll the rail below to walk through it.

02 The departures screen, read closely

One direction at a time, at the nearest stop. Swipe flips between Westbahnhof (H) and Gersthof (R) — the tabs up top track it. When a direction runs from two poles, like line 9's two Gersthof platforms here, they stack, each with its own header and next two. The crown steps to farther stops. 04 Color is the mode

Your idea: the color carries the mode, so a glance says "tram" or "U-Bahn" before you read a word — the tile buttons, and the deep red ground on the line-9 screens. One catch worth knowing: the Wiener Linien feed publishes no colors at all. So this is an in-app map, keyed off the line name for U-Bahn and vehicle.type for the rest.

05 How it maps to the build

The app is the product; the tile is a Phase-3 shortcut on top of it. This model leans hard on location, so Phase 2 is the heart of the app. Phase 0 (the laptop script) already proved every field these screens show.

Phase 1One hardcoded line + stop, live. The departures screen (02), no location yet. Learns the toolchain.
Phase 2The app proper. Home, GPS, the bundled stop CSV, nearby stops, pick a line, swipe-direction departures with stacked poles, crown-scroll to farther stops. GPS also picks the right pole when a stop has two.
Phase 3Favorites + the tile. Pin lines in the app, persist the list, render the honeycomb tile, deep-link each button back into the located departures view.

06 Open questions for you

Decided

↔Home — Nearby in the middle; swipe left/right to favorites and search, leaving up/down to scroll the lists
⌨Search a stop — the watch's built-in text + voice input; no custom keyboard
‹ ›Swipe — flip direction, H ↔ R (one direction on screen at a time)
↻Crown — step to the next stop along the line, farther out
→Platforms — same-direction poles stack with a header each; a single platform skips it
🚆Tile capacity — six pinned lines around a centre app button; tiles can't scroll, so six is the hard cap
Colored background — too much? The line-9 screens sit on a deep tram-red ground. Keep the full tint, or pull it back to just the badge so the times stay on plain black?
A/C snowflake. Show it year-round, or only in warm months when it actually changes your choice?
